Why A is the starting point

A takes only 96 × 28 pt, uses the same glass as the card, and keeps the familiar red–yellow–green order. The 12 pt circles sit inside separate 26 pt hit areas. Hover reveals the symbols and an action label.

Proposed gesture

Red closes the selected window. Yellow minimizes it. Green proposes full screen; the control above also lets you compare Fill display. For the app: hold the middle button, move onto a light, then release. Hover alone does nothing; return to the center ring to cancel.
